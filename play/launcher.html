Your copy

Bring your own Morrowind

Point to the Data Files folder from a legal install. Files stream straight from your disk on demand, with no multi-gigabyte upload or copy. Tribunal, Bloodmoon and any mods (.esp/.esm/.bsa) in the folder are detected and loaded automatically.

Where is it?
Steam  steamapps\common\Morrowind\Data Files
GOG  C:\GOG Games\Morrowind\Data Files
Picking the Morrowind folder itself works too. Chrome will then ask to see the folder — choose View files.

Choose Data Files ▸
Morrowind game data is not included or distributed here. Supply your own legally-obtained copy. openmw-wasm is based on the OpenMW engine, effectively rebuilt in WebAssembly to run in the browser, and is released under the GPLv3. Loading your own files needs a Chromium browser (Chrome, Edge or Brave) on desktop.
