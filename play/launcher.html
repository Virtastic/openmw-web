Your copy

Bring your own Morrowind

Point to the Data Files folder from a legal install. Files stream straight from your disk on demand, with no multi-gigabyte upload or copy. Tribunal, Bloodmoon and any mods (.esp/.esm/.bsa) in the folder are detected and loaded automatically.

Windows + Steam or a Program Files install? The browser can't open system folders, so first copy your Data Files folder to Documents or Desktop and pick that. Same in Chrome and Edge.

Morrowind game data is not included or distributed here. Supply your own legally-obtained copy. openmw-web is based on the OpenMW engine, effectively rebuilt in WebAssembly to run in the browser, and is released under the GPLv3. Loading your own files needs a Chromium browser (Chrome, Edge or Brave) on desktop.
