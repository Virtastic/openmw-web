Bring your own Morrowind

Point to the Data Files folder from a legal install. Files stream straight from your disk on demand, with no multi-gigabyte upload or copy. Tribunal, Bloodmoon and any mods (.esp/.esm/.bsa) in the folder are detected and loaded automatically.
